SwapDay Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This is an English translation of the Korean original. In case of any discrepancy, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information We Collect

None. This app does not directly collect any personal information such as your name, contact details, or location. No sign-up or login is required.

2. How Data Is Stored

The consumable items, replacement cycles, and replacement history you enter are stored only on your device (localStorage) and are never sent to any external server.

3. JSON Export and Import

A file saved with JSON export is stored only in your device's Downloads folder, and an imported file is processed only on your device. Files are never sent to any external server.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

This app itself does not share your information with any third party. However, the Google AdMob SDK is included for advertising, and the information it collects is described in Section 5.

5. Advertising

This app shows a bottom banner ad through Google AdMob. To serve ads, the AdMob SDK may collect the following information.

  • Device or other IDs (advertising ID)
  • IP address (for approximate location)
  • Ad interaction history (such as ad clicks)
  • Diagnostics (such as crash logs)

The collected information may be shared with Google to serve ads and prevent fraud. If you do not want personalized ads, turn off ad personalization in your device settings.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
